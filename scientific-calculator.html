Keyboard and accessibility help

  • Use Tab and Shift + Tab to move through controls. Press Enter or Space to activate buttons.
  • When the page itself has focus, number keys and +, −, *, /, brackets, decimal point and ^ enter an expression.
  • Press Enter to calculate, Backspace to delete the last character and Escape to clear the expression.
  • Use the angle selector to choose DEG or RAD before trigonometric calculations.
  • Voice feedback uses your browser's speech synthesis. A screen reader such as NVDA can also read labelled controls and live results.
